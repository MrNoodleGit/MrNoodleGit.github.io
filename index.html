About

I'm a performer, researcher, contemplative explorer, and experience architect. I was born in the US and grew up in Medellín, Colombia. I graduated from MIT, where I studied Computation and Cognition as well as Theater Arts.

My mission is to investigate both the material and subjective foundations of our world, and to apply that knowledge in the service of compassionate intelligence, more joyful human-computer interactions, and more effective psychiatry. I'm open for professional collaborations, artistic projects, and fun adventures.

I look for bridges between the realms of art, science, and philosophy and the tangible, intimate wisdom of our daily lives. I aspire to tell beautiful and compelling stories about these realms.

I speak English, Spanish, French, and Portuguese. I get along very well with old people and babies. I'm obsessed with mangos, spicy food, and guacamole.

"I am large, I contain multitudes."

Life

01 / 03

Dancing

Skilled movement became an essential part of my life during the pandemic. I've since participated in a variety of workshops and dance studios around the world. I'm especially interested in improvisational genres, such as freestyle hiphop, breaking, house, and contemporary.

02 / 03

Martial arts

For me, martial arts represents respect for one's opponents, humility, fortitude, and elegance. They have taught me how to remain calm under severe physical pressure, how to learn with and teach people of every skill level, and how to handle distress by challenging one's body and embracing discomfort.

03 / 03

Meditation

Mediation is an enormous aspect of my life. It has shown me that we can cultivate a way of relating to our subjective experience that promotes peace and mitigates suffering. I’ve had the privilege of attending several silent 10-day retreats.
