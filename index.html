    MIT

    Gemini Ask ChatGPT

    The people at MIT are undeniably exceptional, though their genius is often misunderstood. Popular culture frames them as effortless prodigies, but the reality on campus is defined far more by relentless curiosity, insane discipline, and sheer problem-solving grit than raw, effortless IQ.

    Raw Cognitive Ability

    • The Baseline: The average student sat near the top of their high school class with near-perfect SAT/ACT scores and stellar math/science credentials.
    • The Outliers: MIT attracts a concentration of global outliers—International Math and Physics Olympiad medalists, teenagers with published research papers, and young prodigies capable of taking graduate-level courses as freshmen.

    What Sets Them Apart

    • Obsessive Curiosity: They don't just learn a subject to pass a test; they dig until they understand every fundamental layer and want to know "what happens next".
    • Relentless Work Ethic: Brilliant ideas on campus rarely come from sudden lightbulb moments. They are usually the result of long hours, dozens of failed attempts, and an extraordinary tolerance for complex, grueling problem sets.
    • Playful Engineering Culture: MIT brilliance comes with a unique brand of humor and practical application, famously visible in their complex, highly technical "hacks" (pranks), like placing a life-sized Apollo lunar module on top of the Great Dome.

    While natural brainpower sets the floor, it's their relentless drive to build, test, fail, and rebuild that truly makes MIT minds stand out.
