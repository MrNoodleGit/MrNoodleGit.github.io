Why this play

A relatively small number of researchers and engineers are building the most powerful AI systems in the world. Their choices will reach everyone, and the work moves faster than anyone can comfortably think it through.

PAUSE is theater made for them. It isn't a lecture or a warning. It's a chance to stop, look at the work from the outside, and talk about it together.

We're building it from conversations with AI researchers, and researcher advisors will check that the science holds up.

The room

Where
Medellín, Colombia
When
December 1–15, 2026
Who
Ra Mour and four writers
Goal
A complete first draft

The first week (Dec 1–8), everyone is in the room working out the story together. In the second, writers draft scenes, in Medellín or from home. Every week, actors in Denver read the new pages aloud over video. It's a working room: mornings writing together, afternoons drafting, homes chosen to keep costs down.

FAQ

Is my gift tax-deductible?
Not yet. RARE isn't a nonprofit, so gifts go to Ra directly. We plan to work with a fiscal sponsor for larger fundraising in 2027.
Is this a vacation?
No. It's a working room with daily writing hours and shared homes chosen to keep costs down. Ra will be in Medellín this winter, and lodging and food there cost less than in most US cities.
What if you don't reach the goal?
The room goes ahead either way; funding decides how many writers can come.
Can I give anonymously?
Yes. Just say so when you give.
Can I help another way?
Yes. Introductions to AI researchers who'd talk with us are just as valuable.
